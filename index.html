Questions

Good to know

Is this made with AI?

Yes, partly. We plan every shot ourselves (beat map, camera angles in 3D, character sheet), then use AI image and animation tools to produce the frames, and edit the result by hand. We label videos as AI-assisted where platforms ask for it.

Can the character look like me?

Yes, from a photo you send and agree to us using. You can also choose a fictional character.

Who owns the finished video?

You get the rights to publish and monetize your video on any platform. We may show short parts in our showreel unless you ask us not to.

Does it work with vocals?

Yes. For songs with singing we animate lip movement to the vocal track. Instrumental tracks get the same beat-matched playing.

How do sponsored placements work with artists?

Brand placements only go into our own videos or into artist videos where the artist agrees. Artists can earn part of the sponsorship.
